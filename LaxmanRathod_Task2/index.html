A Tribute to Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India | 11th President of India

Who Was He?

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam, known widely as Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, was an Indian aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he rose from humble beginnings to become one of the most respected figures in India's scientific and public life.

He played a key role in India's space and missile programs, earning the title "Missile Man of India." Beyond his scientific achievements, he was deeply loved for his simplicity, humility, and his passion for inspiring students and young people across the country. He is often remembered as the "People's President."

Key Milestones & Achievements

  • 1958
    Graduated in Aeronautical Engineering from Madras Institute of Technology
  • 1969
    Joined ISRO and served as Project Director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle, SLV-III
  • 1980
    Successfully launched the Rohini satellite into orbit using an Indian-made rocket
  • 1998
    Played a central role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests as Chief Scientific Adviser
  • 1997
    Awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor
  • 2002
    Became the 11th President of India, serving until 2007
  • 2015
    Passed away on 27 July while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, doing what he loved most — teaching
